    Reconciliation and FAQ 4 answers

    Definitions follow the report: cohort = first successful paid month; age 0 = acquisition month; retention uses paid subscription periods over the original acquired-customer denominator; contribution = net revenue (after discounts and refunds) minus variable costs; LTV = 12 monthly receipts of contribution discounted 1%/month (t = age+1), before CAC; net value subtracts historical average CAC once; payback = first month cumulative undiscounted contribution ≥ CAC. iCAC uses only holdout waves whose 31-day window ended by the as-of cutoff. Forecasts come from the report's hierarchical sBG + Gamma model refitted with seed 20261116 on cells dated through the as-of month only.
